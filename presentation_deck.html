Problem Statement • Problem H4
The Polypharmacy Crisis in Indian Healthcare
Severe drug-drug interactions and duplicate therapies go undetected in high-volume public OPDs.
Public Health Crisis
Real-World Case Study: Chandrapuri District Hospital

A 62-year-old diabetic and hypertensive patient with mild renal impairment visited three different specialists across 6 months, receiving 7 medications:

  • Duplicate NSAIDs: Ibuprofen 400mg + Diclofenac 50mg prescribed concurrently by ortho and dental clinics, triggering GI bleed risk.
  • Contraindicated ACEi + ARB: Ramipril + Telmisartan co-prescribed, doubling acute hyperkalemia and renal failure danger.
  • Renal Ineligibility: Metformin prescribed at full dose despite eGFR < 30 mL/min (fatal lactic acidosis warning).
84% Alert Fatigue Ignore Rate in Western EHRs
40-70% Elderly Patients taking 5+ Medications
Why Existing Systems Fail in India
  • Indian Brand Name Jungle: Over 100,000 branded generic trade names (e.g., Combiflam, Augmentin, Glycomet-GP) confuse standard US/EU EHR databases.
  • Language & Literacy Divide: 80%+ of elderly rural patients cannot comprehend English prescription warnings.
  • Catastrophic Out-of-Pocket Expense: Chronic polypharmacy drains 35% of rural household income on expensive branded formulations.

Multi-Stage Token Pipeline
  • 1. Noise Filtering: Strips route noise (PO, IV, PRN, BD, TDS), dosage frequencies, and package sizes.
  • 2. Dosage Disentanglement: Separates active molecule names from strength indicators (e.g., "Augmentin-625" → Molecule: Augmentin, Strength: 625mg).

  • 4. Levenshtein Spell Tolerance: Corrects doctor typos ("Paracitamol" → Paracetamol; "Atorvstatin" → Atorvastatin).

The "Never Say Safe" Principle

If any medication cannot be positively verified against the certified pharmacopeia, the engine strictly refuses to issue a green "Safe" badge. It explicitly warns: "Safety cannot be verified for unindexed substances."

KDIGO Renal Staging & Threshold Rules

Dynamic dose adjustment evaluated against patient eGFR (mL/min/1.73m²):

  • Stage 3b/4 (eGFR < 45): Metformin max dose reduced to 1000 mg/day.
  • Stage 5 (eGFR < 30): Metformin absolutely contraindicated (lactic acidosis risk).
  • Ciprofloxacin / Enoxaparin / Digoxin: Automatic threshold alerts triggered with exact dosage guidance.

Data Privacy & Clinical Safety Ethics

DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

Designed from first principles to safeguard citizen privacy and data sovereignty:

  • Zero External Data Leakage: Prescription analysis runs 100% offline within the local browser or hospital edge server.
  • No Data Retention: Patient names and identifiers are never stored on public cloud servers.
  • No Third-Party Trackers: Zero ad trackers, telemetry, or third-party cookies.
  • Explicit patient consent banners prior to prescription scanning.
Deterministic Explainability

Unlike LLMs that hallucinate medical dosages, our clinical decision rules are:

  • 100% Auditable: Every alert references specific CDSCO, KDIGO, or British National Formulary guidelines.
  • Zero Hallucination: Unindexed drugs cannot produce invented interaction scores.
  • Pharmacologic Rationale: Doctors receive exact biochemical mechanisms (e.g., CYP3A4 inhibition, competitive COX-1 binding).
Clinical Liability Boundaries

Clear clinical boundaries embedded throughout the UI:

  • Decision support tool, not an autonomous prescribing agent.
  • Empowers the treating physician to exercise final clinical judgment.
  • Provides structured alternative medications when interactions arise.
  • Doctor Discussion Cards facilitate patient-physician shared decision making.
